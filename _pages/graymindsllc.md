## GrayMinds LLC

Gray Minds LLC is the sole proprietorship and consulting entity through which I deliver professional services at the intersection of intelligent automation, human-centered design, and enterprise technology.

With 25+ years of experience in Intelligent Document Processing (IDP) and AI automation — including deep expertise in Tungsten/Kofax platforms and Oracle/PeopleSoft ERP system — I work with organizations to build systems that work for people, not the other way around.

### What I Do

My consulting practice focuses on:

- **IDP & AI Automation** — designing and implementing document processing pipelines that reduce manual effort and improve accuracy
- **Human-Machine Systems** — applying cognitive psychology principles to automation design so that humans and systems collaborate effectively
- **Technical Product Management** — bridging the gap between business requirements and technical implementation for AI and automation initiatives
- **Strategic Advisory** — helping organizations evaluate, select, and deploy intelligent automation platforms

### Background

My approach to technology is grounded in human factors research dating back to work at Georgia Tech's Center for Human-Machine Systems Research, where I studied how people interact with automated systems in high-stakes environments.  That foundation shapes how I approach every engagement: automation should augment human judgment, not replace it without thought.
