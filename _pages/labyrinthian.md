## Labyrinthian

`labyrinthian.net` was my first registered domain.  The name felt right at the time - a place for projects that wound through odd corridors and led somewhere unexpected.  In the era of handles (mine was daedalus at labyrinthian dot net), it seemed like a good expression for a builder of unusual technology.  Spelling the name out over the phone turned a fun handle into a chore, which eventually led to the family domain at grayminds.com.

> **The domain has a second life.**  Today `labyrinthian.net` runs a live application:  [Labyrinthian](/featured/), a vault-backed tabletop RPG dashboard.  The page you are reading is about the original, not the app.  The name came back around, which feels exactly right for a labyrinth.

### The original, roughly 1995 to 2005

This is where I keep the early work:  web experiments, creative projects, and technical tinkering from before frameworks were a thing, before CSS did much of anything useful, and before "user experience" was a job title.

Areas I plan to document as I recover and reconstruct them:

- Early web design and HTML experiments
- Interactive fiction and hypertext projects
- Perl and early CGI scripting work
- Community forums and early social tools
- Early mapping-API and geolocation projects
- Creative writing and worldbuilding from the period

### Why keep it

Some of this work is genuinely embarrassing by modern standards.  That is the point.  It is a record of how the web was built when everyone was figuring it out together, and how my own approach to technology and craft developed over time.

The labyrinth still matters.  You just have to know which paths led somewhere.
