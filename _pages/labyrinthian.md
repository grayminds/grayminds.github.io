## Labyrinthian

`labyrinthian.net` was my first registered domain. The name felt right at the time — a place for projects that wound through odd corridors and led somewhere unexpected. Back in the day of handles (such as daedalus at labyrinthian dot net), it seemed like a good expression for a builder of innovative technology. However, trying to spell out my email and domain address over the phone turned a fun name into a challenge, which eventually led to the family custom domain of grayminds.com

This page is an archive of work from that era: early web experiments, creative projects, and technical tinkering from roughly 1995 to 2005. Most of it was built before frameworks were a thing, before CSS did much of anything useful, and before "user experience" was a job title.

### Why Keep It

Some of this work is genuinely embarrassing by modern standards. That's the point. It is a record of how the web was built when everyone was figuring it out together, and how my own approach to technology and craft developed over time.

The labyrinth still matters. You just have to know which paths led somewhere.
