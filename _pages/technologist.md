## Technologist

I am a Senior Technical Product Manager who builds, deploys, and orchestrates agentic systems and intelligent automation products.  Twenty-five years in enterprise automation, with deep work in intelligent document processing (IDP) on Tungsten and Kofax platforms, and a current practice centered on production agents in Claude Code.

My work lives where automation output meets a real business process.  That is the seam where accuracy thresholds, edge cases, cognitive load, and operational risk all have to be reconciled, and it is the part most automation projects get wrong.

### What that looks like in practice

- **Delivered $750K in cost savings** for a $121B enterprise by engineering an AI/ML/LLM document-management solution, remediating failed extractions and establishing accuracy thresholds for 16 business units.
- **Reduced deployment timelines by 40%** by packaging reusable document-processing components into starter and accelerator kits.
- **Cut RPA costs by 60%** by diagnosing one-off implementations as the root cause and establishing reusable automation principles tied to business KPIs.
- **Build and deploy production agents** in Claude Code today:  writing prompts, reading code, debugging agent behavior, and packaging the patterns into reusable kits with evaluation-driven validation.

### How I work

I validate assumptions early, measure what matters, and keep humans in the loop where judgment counts.  Best practices are the baseline, not the aspiration:  structured logging, least-privilege access, reproducible builds, and tested conventions, even in a one-off script.

The fastest way to understand automation is to live it, so I build tools and workflows for my own use - a self-hosted dashboard that watches every project, a vault-backed application that runs a live game, and a skill family that gives an agent a voice on a token budget.  Those builds are on the [projects](/projects/) page, and the full history is on my [CV](/cv/).
