## How I Use AI

I collaborate with AI tools in my professional and creative work.  My primary collaborator is Claude by Anthropic.  I also use Google's Gemini, NotebookLM, and Nano Banana 2 as well as OpenAI's ChatGPT.  I also run several local models through LM Studio for non-cloud privacy.

AI assists me with drafting, research, editorial review, code generation, and data structuring.  It does not replace my judgment, my expertise, or my voice.  Every piece of AI-assisted content I publish goes through a deliberate review process:

1. I verify all factual claims and technical details independently.
2. I ensure the final text reflects my perspective and intended meaning.
3. I confirm that the tone, structure, and conclusions are authentically mine.

I disclose AI involvement on each post where it played a meaningful role.  I do this because I believe transparency builds trust, and trust is earned through honesty about process.

## Beyond Content

AI involvement in this site goes beyond writing.  I use AI tools for content management and workflow automation, including the systems that organize, format, publish, and maintain what you see here.

This includes building and maintaining the publish pipeline that syncs content from my personal knowledge base to this site, automating file conversion and metadata management, generating scripts for batch processing, and structuring data files that drive site features.  The site itself, its templates, styling customizations, and configuration, was built collaboratively with AI assistance.

In other words, AI is not just a drafting tool.  It is part of the operational infrastructure behind this site.  The same transparency standard applies: I direct the work, review the output, and take responsibility for the result.

I take full responsibility for everything I publish, regardless of how it was created.  

If you have questions about my AI practices, I welcome the conversation.
