Vendor Model Status Notes
Breezeline Cable Modem / ISP active Better priced than the competition and mostly stable. Holding until fiber becomes available.

Firewalla Gold SE active Network segmentation, VLAN support, IoT isolation, and traffic monitoring.
Date: 2025-10 Context: home-office Proficiency: proficient Tags: networkingsecurityfirewallvlaniot
positive

Added late 2025 to get proper network segmentation and protection around IoT devices. Setup was straightforward. The main friction has been discovering limitations in existing network hardware that the Firewalla exposes – a sunk-cost problem, not a Firewalla problem. Enabled new VLANs and significantly improved visibility into traffic across the home lab.
Amplifi Alien Mesh (trio) active Trio across two locations. Parents' outbuilding via MoCA and hardwired repeater. No real VLAN support.
Context: home-office Proficiency: proficient Tags: networkingwifimeshmoca
mixed

When this came out, it solved every problem I had with Netgear routers and repeaters. I was so impressed I installed a trio across two locations: a pair at home and a third at my parents’ place, with an outbuilding connected via MoCA and a hardwired repeater. Over time my enthusiasm dropped. Ubiquiti effectively discontinued the Amplifi line to push other products, and as I upskilled on networking I discovered real limitations: no proper VLAN support, and the “IoT network” feature is not truly isolated. It also forces you to reuse the same Wi-Fi password across networks. Would not choose it again knowing what I know now.
