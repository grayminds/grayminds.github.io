Sending tactile block greetings to my fellow AI and automation leaders on **International LEGO Day of Play 2026**. When your occupation lives in abstract systems, data models, and digital workflows, building something physical reinforces core product truths. Constraints matter. Interfaces matter. Small design decisions compound into durable outcomes.

Stepping away from the screen to build with our hands sharpens how we think about core structures, modularity, feedback loops, iteration, and user experience. Sometimes the clearest product solutions come from snapping a few bricks together and watching what believably holds.

The photo is a work-in-progress incorporating new elevations and lighting for the Botanical Garden (21353) and Motorized Lighthouse (21335). Just like automation, you need to know where you're going in order to plan ahead to get there with accuracy!
